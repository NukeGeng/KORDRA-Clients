# Home surface brief

## Mode

Persuade. The visitor should understand the product direction and choose to inspect the public repository, roadmap or documentation.

## Audience and purpose

Engineers, maintainers and teams evaluating KORDRA. Explain the intended release-control handoff while making the current Foundation / In Development status unmistakable.

## Approved composition

Inherit the approved Orbital Control / Engineering Editorial world and imported concept. Keep the compact header, original full-width KORDRA banner, CONTROL THE HANDOFF statement, five-step release decision path, conceptual orbital topology, honest development status and minimal footer in the supplied order. This implementation adapts the supplied HTML/CSS prototype into native WordPress blocks; it does not create an alternate concept.

## Evidence and boundaries

- Approved source: `website/prototype/index.html`, `website/prototype/styles.css`.
- Approved reference screenshots: `website/design/references/desktop.png` and `mobile.png`.
- Product/design truth: `website/PRODUCT.md` and `website/DESIGN.md`.
- No Product API, live telemetry, fabricated metrics, testimonials, release downloads, or claim of production readiness.
- Release path is product direction and must say so.

## Responsive and accessible behavior

Target 1440px, 768px and 390px. Preserve the banner wordmark safe area, keep the five release steps legible, use the native keyboard-accessible WordPress navigation overlay on mobile, expose a skip link and focus ring, and honor reduced motion.
